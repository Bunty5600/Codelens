# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in CodeLens AI to be a respectful, constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, and other abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Include relevant context or links when possible. The maintainer will review reports and take appropriate action.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project welcoming and productive.
